Master Fleet Agreement

1. Commercial Tariff Structure

Each approved EV scooter allocation is subject to a daily non-refundable rental fee of ₹100.00, debited from the rider's digital wallet when an administrator approves the request.

2. Hub Check-Out Obligation

Riders must return vehicles to authorized hub parking bays and click "Return Vehicle & Check Out" to cease billing and release the scooter for charging.

3. Vehicle Damage & Compliance

Riders agree to operate within municipal traffic laws. Physical damage resulting from reckless driving or abandonment will incur maintenance penalty charges.

4. Rider Document Verification

Before requesting a scooter, riders must upload a driving licence, Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and passport-size photo. Each document must be 100 MB or smaller and in JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF format. An administrator must approve the documents and separately approve each vehicle allocation request before a scooter is assigned.
